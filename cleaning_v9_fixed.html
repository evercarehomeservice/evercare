FAQ

Questions we hear
before the first clean.

How much does home cleaning cost in Worcester, MA?

Regular cleaning starts at $120. A typical 3-bedroom home runs $150–$180. Deep cleans from $180. Move-in/out from $200. Text us for a same-day quote with no pressure.

Why does EverCare cost more than the cheapest option I found online?

The cheapest services rotate strangers through your home, skip background checks, and have no one accountable when something goes wrong. At EverCare you get one consistent professional — CORI-verified, fully insured — who learns your home and your standards over time. The price reflects accountability, not just hours. Most of our clients tell us the peace of mind alone is worth more than saving $20 a visit.

Will I get the same cleaner every time?

Yes — always. We assign one dedicated cleaner to your home. They learn your space, your preferences, and your standards. No strangers rotating through.

Are your cleaners CORI background-checked?

Yes. Every EverCare professional passes Massachusetts' CORI criminal background check before entering any client's home. Fully insured and bonded.

What if something isn't cleaned to my standards?

We come back and redo it — free of charge, no questions asked. Text Renan directly and we'll be back within 48 hours to make it right.

What makes EverCare different from a franchise like Molly Maid?

The main difference is consistency. Franchises rotate staff — you rarely get the same person twice. At EverCare you get one dedicated cleaner who learns your home. You can text Renan directly, not a call center. If something's not right, we come back within 48 hours free.
